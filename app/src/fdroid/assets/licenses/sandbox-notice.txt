Source distribution
-------------------
Look for gpl-sources-v<app-version>-corrected.tar.gz beside the matching APK:
https://github.com/chloemeadow0-code/Orange-Island-Releases/releases
The archive should include the exact Alpine package recipes, their source
archives and patches, PRoot/talloc sources, and the Android build script.
A local source package or an upstream link does not prove that a matching
release artifact is available. If the source for your version is missing,
report the version at:
https://github.com/chloemeadow0-code/Orange-Island-Releases/issues
